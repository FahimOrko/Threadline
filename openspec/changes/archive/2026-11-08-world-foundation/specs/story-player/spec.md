## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Data sources note mentions Impact World
The report's data-sources note SHALL say that equivalences such as trees appear in Impact World as labelled illustrations, and SHALL NOT say that equivalences are not shown. The same note in the export SHALL read the same.

#### Scenario: Note text
- **WHEN** the methodology chapter or the export is rendered
- **THEN** the data-sources note says trees appear in Impact World as labelled illustrations
